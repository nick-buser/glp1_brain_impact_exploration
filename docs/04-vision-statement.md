# GLP-1 Brain Mechanism Atlas — Vision

## What it is

A mechanism atlas for the GLP-1 receptor agonist literature: an interactive cognitive workbench that lets a sophisticated reader move between clean mechanism views, coupled-system overlays, evidence-grounded claim graphs, uncertainty modes, and phenomenology mappings — so the literature becomes something they can inspect and reason with, not merely something they read.

## Why it needs to exist

The GLP-1RA literature is one of the most consequential pieces of contemporary neuropharmacology and one of the most badly served by the registers in which it currently circulates. Pop-science flattens it ("Ozempic makes dopamine go down"). Review articles fog it (forty pages, no structural anchor). Pharmacovigilance simplifies it (FAERS signal, EMA review, headline). Clinical phenomenology disconnects it from mechanism ("Ozempic personality"). Each register collapses lenses the literature actually requires the reader to hold separately: rodent central acute is not human peripheral chronic; wanting is not liking; satiety is not aversive interoception; observational EHR signal is not RCT outcome; receptor anatomy is not BBB pharmacokinetics is not circuit-level mechanism. The literature looks more contested than it is, and is more nuanced than any one register admits.

The atlas exists to make that nuance *inhabitable*. To give a reader a structure they can hold, manipulate, return to, and update as the evidence moves.

## What it is for

It is for the moment when a thoughtful reader wants to understand what a chronic, peripheral, pharmacological agonist of a normally phasic and aversive interoceptive system actually does to a human brain over years — and finds that the question is not answerable from any single source they have access to.

It is for the moment when a new paper lands on their queue and they need to know *where it sits* before they need to know what it says.

It is for the moment when they catch themselves about to repeat a pop-science collapse and want a substrate that makes the collapse visibly wrong.

It is for the moment when they want to think honestly about emotional blunting, alcohol craving reduction, the EVOKE failure, or the wanting/liking distinction, and they want a structure that holds the uncertainty without dissolving it.

## What it is not

Not a chatbot. Not a medical advice tool. Not a literature search engine. Not a dashboard. Not a definitive reference. Not a "what Ozempic does" explainer. Not a snapshot of mid-2026 thinking that quietly diverges from the territory.

## The deepest commitment

That the atlas is honest under load. That every claim carries its confidence and its provenance. That scope conditions — species, route, dose, chronicity — are always visible at the point where they matter. That contradictions render as structure rather than hedging. That open questions are first-class entities and not the conclusion of an article. That the artifact is maintained, not published once. That the design conveys honest unsettledness without losing aesthetic discipline. That participation is preferred over delegation; the reader does the reasoning, the atlas makes the reasoning cheap.

## The trophic role in the larger ecosystem

A decomposer-by-shape over a tangled literature: papers can be placed against an existing structure rather than re-engaged from scratch.

A pollinator between specialist registers: rodent neuropharmacology, human fMRI, RCT epidemiology, clinical phenomenology each translate into the others through a shared mechanism graph.

An apex consumer at the highest-effort surfaces: contradiction views, evidence-confidence overlays, moderator dashboards perform pattern recognition over the literature that no off-the-shelf tool produces.

An interlocutor at the phenomenology mapper: half-articulated subjective reports get returned to the reader as *structure to think with*, not as answers to accept.

A prototype for a reusable cognitive fixture for any literature where linear prose collapses under multi-mechanism causal entanglement.

## The success condition, in one sentence

A reader who arrives confused leaves with sharper lens discipline, an updated sense of what is contested and what is settled, the lens-switching habit installed, and a substrate they want to return to as new evidence accumulates.

## The failure condition, in one sentence

A reader skims a pretty review article, learns nothing they could not have learned from a podcast, and never returns.

## The single sharpest framing

This is a cultivated cognitive prosthesis at the apex-synthesis-and-decomposer level over a specific knowledge domain, sitting on a small set of native-flora libraries, whose value depends entirely on the honesty and stewardship of its claim graph — and whose central design move is making lens-switching explicit, evidence interrogable, scope conditions visible, and contradictions first-class.

If we get that move right, everything else follows.
